Happy Thanksgiving

Grateful for every
independent contractor

     

As Thanksgiving approaches, all of us at NICA wanted to pause and say something we don’t say often enough: thank you. 🍁

Being an independent contractor takes grit. You set your own schedule, carry your own risk, and show up for your customers every single day, often without anyone noticing the long hours behind the work. You are the backbone of the businesses you serve, and we see it.

It’s a privilege to stand alongside you. Whether it’s settlement services, insurance, or tax time, our job is to make sure that while you work independently, you never have to go it alone.

This year, we’re especially grateful for the trust you place in us. We hope you get some well-earned time off to rest, enjoy a great meal, and be with the people who matter most. 🥧
